and unique typed targets. The two2050 sources require three tagged links;
the two55 sources repeat a typed group target; the two57 sources require

not a claim that all producer behavior in these sources was previously broken.

Any truncation at 2,048 route cells, 16 candidates per edge, or 512 candidates total, and any 65,536-step selection or work exhaustion, must fail with a resource-classified result. [flow400_resource](/Users/zzzode/Develop/Loom/tests/support/mermaid_flow_oracle.hpp:1172) must also recognize the existing `GroupEndpoint route candidate bound` and `GroupEndpoint exclusive route ownership exhausted` messages. - Missing, duplicate, and reused heads; shared source or target ports and route cells; branches, crossings, and orphan arms.
- Wrong typed endpoints, membership, source-order partition, and direction where rank is defined, including ambiguous route swaps that might otherwise conceal a wrong endpoint.
- Exact source pins, following-row behavior, and legacy default rejection.
- Each route-length, per-edge candidate, total-candidate, selection, and work limit, including recognition of the legacy resource messages and absence of semantic-negative credit.
